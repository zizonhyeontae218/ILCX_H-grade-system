# Branding and notation

Canonical notation:

**ILCX 4H+ grade**

Rules:

- `ILCX` is uppercase.
- No hyphen between the numeral and `H`.
- `+` immediately follows `H`.
- `grade` is lowercase in the canonical English form.
- A project name may precede or follow the grade, e.g. `ILCX - CometDust — ILCX 4H+ grade`.

The mark communicates human contribution only. Do not present it as a government certification, third-party audit, or legal trademark registration unless that status actually exists.
